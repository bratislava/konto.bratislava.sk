# Mailgun templates → emails in this package

Where each Mailgun template went, and every intentional change against it. The
`digital.bratislava.sk` and `test.bratislava.sk` copies are identical, so the domain is only named for
templates from other domains.

All emails share the new layout, so these changes apply to every row and are not repeated:

- One design: city layout, Inter (Arial where clients don't load web fonts), dark mode, partner logos
  next to the city's, the brand colour on buttons.
- Subjects come from the email, with a product prefix (`Bratislavské konto:`, `OLO:`, `TSB:`, `PAAS:`,
  `Kúpaliská:`). Internal emails keep the subject the caller passes.
- Footer: "Ďakujeme, že využívate naše služby." + "Vaše Hlavné mesto SR Bratislava" (partners:
  "Vaše OLO &", "Vaše TSB &", "Váš PAAS &", "Váš STARZ &"), privacy notice, © year.
- Copy: "e-mail", capitalised Vás/Váš, greeting "Dobrý deň, {meno}," or "Dobrý deň," without a name.
- Plain-text version and inbox preview text for every email.
